# Istignet (Istigkeit + Network)

> **Istignet** (*Istigkeit* + *Network*): Combining Aldous Huxley’s borrowed philosophical term for the pure “Is-ness” or essence of things with the structure of a decentralized network. It signifies a web of raw, unfiltered, and uncorrupted knowledge.

## Founding Mission Statement

> “Our mission is to bypass the artificial filters of traditional education and connect learners directly to the boundless intelligence of the global community. We believe that knowledge, much like the Mind at Large, should never be confined, rationed, or gated by proprietary walls. We are building a decentralized, open-source ecosystem where information flows freely, empowering every individual to tap into the unadulterated reality of collective human discovery and contribute to a limitless shared consciousness.”
