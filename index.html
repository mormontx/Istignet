Stage I. Classical Computation, Reversible Logic & Shannon Information Theory [Load Stage I Concept Map ↑]

Classical Core: Turing machines, Boolean circuit complexity, Landauer’s erasure bound ($k_B T \ln 2$), Toffoli/Fredkin reversible logic, Shannon entropy $H(X)$, Kullback–Leibler divergence $D(p\parallel q)$, and the Noisy Channel Coding Theorem.
Quantum Information Bridge: Why unitary evolution demands physical reversibility; Bennett’s uncomputation trick to clear ancilla garbage before quantum interference; $H(X)$ as the diagonal spectrum limit of Von Neumann entropy $S(\rho)$.
Primary Monographs: Elements of Information Theory (Cover & Thomas); Lectures on Computation (Feynman); Information Theory, Inference, and Learning Algorithms (MacKay).

Stage II. Finite-Dimensional Hilbert Spaces, Quantum Postulates & Non-Local Games [Load Stage II Concept Map ↑]

Mathematical Substrate: Spectral Theorem for normal operators, tensor products $\mathcal{H}_A \otimes \mathcal{H}_B$, Singular Value & Schmidt Decomposition, projective vs. POVM measurements.
Information-Theoretic Focus: No-Cloning & No-Signaling duality; Bell inequalities framed as the CHSH cooperative information game (classical $75\%$ vs. Tsirelson $\cos^2(\pi/8) \approx 85.4\%$ quantum limit).
Primary Monographs: Quantum Computation and Quantum Information (Nielsen & Chuang); The Theory of Quantum Information (Watrous).

Stage III. Quantum Circuits, Oracular Complexity & Algorithmic Speedups [Load Stage III Concept Map ↑]

Algorithmic Spine: Universal gate sets, Solovay–Kitaev theorem, phase kickback, Deutsch–Jozsa, Bernstein–Vazirani, Simon’s periodicity, Quantum Fourier Transform, Shor’s factoring, and Grover’s $\mathcal{O}(\sqrt{N})$ optimality proof via the hybrid method.
Primary Monographs: Quantum Computing Since Democritus (Aaronson); Classical and Quantum Computation (Kitaev, Shen & Vyalyi).

Stage IV. Quantum Information Theory: Channels, Entropy & Entanglement Resource Theory [Load Stage IV Concept Map ↑]

Open Systems & Capacities: Density operators $\rho$, purifications, CPTP quantum channels, Kraus & Stinespring dilation, Von Neumann entropy $S(\rho) = -\operatorname{Tr}(\rho \log_2 \rho)$, Strong Subadditivity, Holevo’s bound $\chi$, Schumacher compression, and LOCC entanglement distillation.
Primary Monographs: Quantum Information Theory (Mark Wilde); The Theory of Quantum Information (John Watrous); Preskill Caltech Lecture Notes.

Stage V. Quantum Error Correction, Complexity Theory ($\mathsf{BQP}/\mathsf{QMA}$) & Thesis Monograph [Load Stage V Concept Map ↑]

Fault Tolerance & Complexity: Pauli stabilizer formalism, Gottesman–Knill theorem, Shor 9-qubit & Steane 7-qubit codes, topological surface codes, fault-tolerant threshold theorem, and $\mathsf{QMA}$-completeness of the $k$-Local Hamiltonian problem.
Primary Monographs: Stabilizer Codes and Quantum Error Correction (Gottesman PhD Thesis); Quantum Hamiltonian Complexity (Gharibian et al.).
